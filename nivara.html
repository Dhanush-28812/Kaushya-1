Nivara

Harvesting cocoons without hours of hand picking.

What it is

Nivara is our harvesting machine. It removes cocoons from the plastic mountage so farmers do not have to pick them by hand.

Current design direction

  • The plastic mountage is placed on a frame with triangular ridges, so it folds evenly.
  • A second panel with matching triangles presses it from above, like a scanner lid.
  • A scraper plate with the same triangles is pushed across by hand and removes the cocoons.
  • Cocoons are collected in a tray.

Where we are

We changed this design after testing ideas against how the plastic mountage actually folds. We are now building and testing the scraper version.
