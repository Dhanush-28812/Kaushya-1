Cocoon sorter

Grading cocoons consistently with colour sensors.

What it is

A sensor-based sorter. TCS34725 colour sensors read each cocoon as it passes through, and the sorter decides which grade it belongs to.

Why colour sensors

  • Every cocoon is judged the same way.
  • It is faster than sorting by eye.
  • The sensors are small and low-cost, so the machine stays affordable for farmers.
