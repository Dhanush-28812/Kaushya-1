Cocoon sorter

Grading cocoons consistently with a camera.

What it is

A camera-based sorter. A small camera module looks at each cocoon and decides which grade it belongs to.

Why camera-based

  • It judges every cocoon the same way.
  • It is faster than sorting by eye.
  • The hardware is small and low-cost.
