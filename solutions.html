Our solutions

One tool for each stage of the pre-cocoon process.

Aakar

Collapsible plastic mountage with hexagonal cells, built for uniform cocoons.

Details

Nivara

Harvesting machine that removes cocoons from the mountage with brushes and a scraper.

Details

Cocoon sorter

Colour-sensor system (TCS34725) that checks each cocoon and sorts it into grades.

Details

How they fit together

Aakar shapes the cocoons well. Nivara removes them from Aakar cleanly. The sorter then grades what Nivara collects. Each tool also works on its own.

Status: all three are in the prototyping phase.
