About us

Students building practical tools for silk farmers.

Who we are

Team Kaushya is a group of students from Wisdom World School, Wakad. We work on the pre-cocoon stage of sericulture, the part of silk farming where cocoons are mounted, harvested and sorted.

Why we started

Much of this work is still done by hand. We saw that farmers lose time and money on it, and that small design changes could help. So we set out to build simple, low-cost tools that farmers can afford and repair.

What we are entering

We are taking part in a national-level innovation and startup competition under the theme Empowering Hands.
