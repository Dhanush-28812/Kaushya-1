The problem

Where sericulture farmers lose time and money.

Three linked steps

After silkworms spin, the work moves through mounting (chandrika), harvesting and sorting. A weakness at one stage makes the next one harder.

MountingCocoons form in uneven shapes and sizes on the mountage.
HarvestingCocoons are picked off by hand, which is slow and tiring.
SortingGrading by eye is inconsistent and takes time.

What it costs

Farmers we spoke to pay for hired labour during harvesting. We estimate this at about ₹30,000 per year for one farm.
