Aakar

A collapsible mountage that keeps the hexagon.

What it is

Aakar is a redesigned plastic mountage (chandrika). It keeps the hexagonal cell shape that silkworms and farmers already know, but it folds flat.

How it works

  • Flat panels with honeycomb-shaped cuts.
  • Hinge-type links join the panels.
  • The panels sit on a frame with triangular ridges and take its triangular shape.
  • Taken off the frame, the mountage folds flat for storage and transport.

Why it matters

  • More uniform cocoon size and shape.
  • Easy to store between seasons.
  • Plastic is easy to clean.
